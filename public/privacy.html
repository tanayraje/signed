Privacy policy

Signed, last updated 31 October 2026

Signed is a small app for writing letters to friends. This page explains what it keeps and why.

What Signed keeps

  • Your name, email and Google account ID, so you can sign in.
  • Your seal and owl choices.
  • Your last location, rounded to a nearby city, saved when you open the app. It's used to work out how far a letter flies, and only friends you've connected with can see it.
  • Letters, scraps, photos, voice and video notes you send, and the friends you connect with.
  • A notification token for your device, if you turn on notifications.

Who can see it

Letters are only visible to the person who sent them and the friend they were sent to, and only once they've landed. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Services Signed uses

Supabase stores the data. Vercel hosts the app. Google handles sign-in. If you record a talking letter, the recording may be sent to OpenAI to turn it into words.

Deleting your data

Email tanayraje99@gmail.com and your account and letters will be deleted.
